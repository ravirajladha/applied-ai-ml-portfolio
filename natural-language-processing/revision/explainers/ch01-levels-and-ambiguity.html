1 · What NLP even is

Analogy. Learning a foreign language, you don't just memorise a dictionary. You need to know how words are built (walk → walked), which strings are actually words (friendship yes, beautyship no), what order they go in, what they mean together, whether saying it makes sense here, and what the previous sentence was about. Those six things are exactly the six levels of language analysis — the course's spine.

NLP = analyse, understand and generate human languages just like humans do. Started as a branch of AI; borrows from Linguistics, Psycholinguistics, Cognitive Science and Statistics.

The slogan on the slide: make computers learn our language rather than we learn theirs.

2 · ⭐ The six levels — climb the ladder

Bottom to top. Click each rung. Every rung assumes the ones below it already work.

The pattern to notice. Each classic example sentence is correct at the level below and broken at the level above. So if an exam hands you a weird sentence and asks "what's wrong with it?", just walk up the ladder until something breaks. That's the whole technique.

4 · ⭐ Three kinds of ambiguity

Analogy. "I saw her duck." Did you watch a bird she owns, or watch her crouch? Nothing is misspelled, nothing is ungrammatical — the sentence is simply two sentences wearing the same coat. English does this constantly, and every one of the hard problems in this course exists because of it.

Exam trap — polysemy vs homonymy. Both are lexical ambiguity.
Polysemy = one word, several related senses (mouse the rodent → mouse the device, named by resemblance).
Homonymy = same form, unrelated senses that just collided (bank the riverside vs bank the institution — different origins entirely).

5 · Evaluation — the contingency table, live

Analogy. A metal detector on a beach. Precision = of everything you dug up, how much was actually treasure. Recall = of all the treasure that was buried, how much did you find. Turn the sensitivity up and recall rises while precision falls; turn it down and the reverse. F1 is the score that refuses to let you cheat on either one.

TN is unknowable here without a fixed candidate pool — which is exactly why extraction tasks report P/R/F1 rather than accuracy.

Why the harmonic mean and not the average? Because it punishes imbalance. A system with P = 1.0 and R = 0.0 has an arithmetic mean of 0.5 — which looks respectable — but F1 = 0, which is the truth. Try the "Cautious" preset and watch F1 sit well below the average of the two.

6 · Pre-processing pipeline

Click each stage to apply it to the raw text.

Stemming vs lemmatization. Stemming chops affixes with crude rules and can produce a non-word: studies → studi. Lemmatization uses a dictionary and returns a real form: studies → study. Stemming is fast and sloppy; lemmatization is slower and correct.
